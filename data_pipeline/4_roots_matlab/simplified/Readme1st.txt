This folder contains a simplified version of the Lindsey-Fox grid-seach-in-polar-coordinates polynomial factorization method. It is much slower than lroots because it does not use any mex files.

These are the only programs not written by James W. Fox. They are not supported.

t_examples.m draws several of the plots from the paper, "Factoring Very High Degree Polynomials" in the November 2003 issue of "Signal Processing Magazine." This program may take a minute to finish. Please be patient.
